  • Plug the board into this computer with a USB cable that carries data; some cables only charge.
  • If its screen is dark, touch it or press BOOT: a sleeping board may not show up.
  • Close anything else that may hold the port: mpremote, Thonny, a serial monitor, another tab with this page.
  • Still missing? Start it in download mode. Waveshare's way: hold BOOT, then press PWR to power on.
