Wi-Fi

The board joins your network over Bluetooth. Its app offers this by itself while it has no network; otherwise turn on “Wi-Fi setup” on its radio page.

The board is not in the list
  • Plug the board into this computer with a USB cable that carries data; some cables only charge.
  • If its screen is dark, touch it or press BOOT: a sleeping board may not show up.
  • Close anything else that may hold the port: mpremote, Thonny, a serial monitor, another tab with this page.
  • An app on the board may have taken its serial port off USB. Start it in download mode: switch it off (hold PWR), then hold BOOT and short-press PWR. It shows up as “USB JTAG/serial debug unit”.
